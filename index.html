    War & chain register 
    
                        
    Every chain Torn has on record for us, newest first. Put each one in exactly one place: a war, a chain event (a chain done instead of a war), or Ignore. Because a chain can only live in one place it is never counted twice — not by two wars, not by the war rule and the payout calculator. Once a war has chains assigned, those are its chain hits for the 3-of-5 rule and the payout calculator; a chain event takes one of the last 5 slots like a war does. Chains you haven't placed yet show a suggested war (the one it overlapped most) with an Accept button.
